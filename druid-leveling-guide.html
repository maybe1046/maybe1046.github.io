Rotations by Level Bracket

1–9 · Caster

  1. Wrath → Moonfire → Wrath until it reaches you
  2. Melee / Wrath to finish
  3. Keep Mark of the Wild + Thorns up; Rejuvenation to top off

10–19 · Bear

  1. Pull: Wrath → Moonfire → shift Bear
  2. Enrage if low rage, Demoralizing Roar on 2+ mobs
  3. Spam Maul; Bash casters; Swipe on packs (16+)
  4. Heal: shift out → Regrowth + Rejuvenation → back in

20–31 · Cat (no Bite yet)

  1. Prowl → Rake (24+) or Claw
  2. Claw to 3–5 combo points
  3. Rip — your only finisher until 32
  4. Ranged pull: Faerie Fire (Feral). Shred only when behind.
  5. 30: Feral Charge (Cat leap / Bear interrupt). 2+ dangerous mobs → Bear.

32–49 · Cat (Bite & Pounce)

  1. Prowl → Pounce (36+) or Ravage from behind
  2. Rake → Claw to 5 CP
  3. Normal mobs: Ferocious Bite at 4–5 CP
  4. Elites: Rake → build → Rip → build → Bite
  5. Tiger's Fury every pull when energy is low
  6. 40: Improved Leader of the Pack = crit self-heals, almost no downtime

50–74 · Cat (Mangle, Berserk)

  1. Open: Pounce or Faerie Fire (Feral)
  2. Mangle → Rake → Mangle/Shred (behind) to 5 CP
  3. Normal: Bite · Elite: Rip then Bite
  4. Berserk (60) on 3+ mob pulls or elites
  5. 62: King of the Jungle → Tiger's Fury gives 60 energy. Maim for runners/casters.
  6. 65+ (Furor): powershift at 0–10 energy (shift out/in = +40 energy)

75–80 · Cat (Savage Roar)

  1. Faerie Fire (Feral) / Pounce
  2. Mangle → Savage Roar at 1–2 CP
  3. Rake → Shred/Mangle to 5 CP
  4. Rip → Bite only when Rip & Roar have >8s left
  5. Trash: skip Rip — Roar then Bite at 5 CP
  6. Omen of Clarity (78) proc → spend on Shred/Mangle, never Bite

AoE & Dungeon Tanking (Dire Bear)

  1. Pull: Feral Charge (Bear) or Faerie Fire (Feral)
  2. Spam Swipe (Bear), Maul on spare rage, keep Demoralizing Roar up, Mangle (Bear) on cooldown
  3. 66+: keep Lacerate on the main target
  4. Loose mob: Growl; whole group loose: Challenging Roar
  5. 71+ Cat AoE: Swipe (Cat) + Berserk

Gear & Stat Priority

Cat

Agility > Strength = Attack Power > Hit (263 rating cap at 80) > Expertise > Crit > Armor Penetration

Bear

Stamina & Armor > Agility. No Defense needed — Survival of the Fittest makes you crit-immune.

  • Wear Agility/Strength leather. Weapon DPS does not matter for ferals — only the stats on it, so pick the 2H staff/mace with the most Agility.
  • Heirlooms (Agility/AP shoulders, chest, staff/polearm) are the biggest XP boost.
  • Professions: Skinning + Leatherworking (self-made gear, drums) or Herbalism (Lifeblood) + Alchemy.

Warmane Icecrown Tips

  • Icecrown runs boosted XP — you outlevel zones fast. Don't full-clear zones; quest on the way and keep a tank queue up for near-instant dungeon pops.
  • You can bear-tank dungeons from ~15 (Ferocity + Feral Instinct help Swipe). From 40, Dire Bear + Survival of the Fittest make you solid.
  • Get Dual Spec at 40: one Cat, one Bear.
